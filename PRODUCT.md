# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single folder. No build step, no dependencies, deployable by opening `index.html` or dropping the folder on any static host. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Casual Indonesian-language web visitors who arrive from a social link, a group chat, or a friend's share, usually on a phone, in a spare two minutes. They are not studying and not being assessed: they want a quick, funny, ego-stroking distraction they can finish and send to someone else. Their job-to-be-done is "give me something fun to do right now that I can share."

## Product Purpose

A quiz website for casual entertainment quizzes — personality quizzes, pop-culture trivia, "which X are you" tests. The landing page exists to get a visitor from curiosity to a playable quiz in one tap, and then to make finishing and sharing feel like the obvious next move. Success means plays started from the landing page, quizzes completed, and results shared.

## Positioning

Not an exam tool and not a creator platform: the differentiator is that the fun starts before any account, install, or sign-up — a visitor can play a real quiz inside the landing page itself, and every quiz ends in a result built to be shared as an image or link. The landing page demonstrates the mechanism (playable quiz) rather than describing it.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive cold from social/messaging links on mobile data. Quizzes are short (roughly 5–12 questions), single-sitting, image- or text-choice based. Results are a title plus a short description, designed to be screenshotted or shared. Sessions are brief and frequently interrupted; nothing should require loading a heavy page or waiting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed for this surface: a landing page with a working in-page quiz demo, category browsing, and a shareable result state.
- Language: Bahasa Indonesia for all visitor-facing copy.
- No backend is confirmed. The in-page demo runs entirely client-side on authored quiz data.
- No account system is confirmed; nothing may require login.
- Terminology: "kuis" (quiz), "mulai" (start), "hasil" (result), "bagikan" (share).

## Brand Commitments

- Visual identity is pinned by the user: the "Caldera" style reference — warm limestone/pumice canvas, ember orange `#fc5000`, plasma violet `#524ae9`, sulfur `#f5f28e`, obsidian near-black text, flat and shadowless, condensed ultrabold display type at architectural scale, halftone dot motif, triple-radius system (100px inputs / 40px cards / 800px pills). This is binding, not a suggestion.
- Voice: playful, fast, a little cheeky, never academic.

## Product Principles

1. The quiz is the pitch — a visitor plays before they are asked to do anything.
2. Two minutes to fun: no sign-up, no install, no wait.
3. Every result is designed to be shared; the ending is a beginning.
4. Fast on a phone on a bad connection.
5. Playful in Bahasa Indonesia, never formal or clinical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Indonesian-language, mobile-first, thumb-reachable controls. No formal standard was specified; build to WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard operability as the baseline.
